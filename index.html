04 / BUILT AROUND YOUR REALITY

Your fleet.
Your systems.
Your missing piece.

We already have booking software. Why CROGE?

The starting point is the gap between your existing tools and your actual workflow: interpreting inquiries, connecting conversations, checking separate schedules or managing follow-ups. If configuration solves it, custom software may not be needed.

Will AI confirm bookings on its own?

The initial scope prepares drafts for staff review. Offers, temporary holds and confirmed bookings remain separate. Availability and pricing are rechecked before any permitted hold or booking action.

What does a focused first version include?

One inquiry channel, structured guest requirements, one approved data source, boat matching, draft quotations, staff handover and follow-up tasks. Additional channels can follow after the workflow proves useful.

What if our availability isn’t live?

A draft assistant can work from an approved export, but it must flag availability for staff verification. Live availability and automatic booking require suitable vendor access and reliable data.

Who is this a good fit for?

Operators with several boats, regular direct inquiries and staff preparing tailored offers. The strongest starting point is a specific, recurring task your current setup does not handle well.
